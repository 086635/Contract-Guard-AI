## 1. 投影规则

`ContractProjector` 集中处理：

- `fileKey = storage_path`
- `fileType` 从文件后缀推导
- `typeConfidence = 1.0`
- `version = contracts.version`
- `currentVersionId = contract_id`
- `missingClauses = []`
- `reportId` 取当前最新报告
- elements 由 `ContractChunk` 投影
- healthScore 由风险和矛盾的等级扣分计算
